Client Profile & Initial State

The client operates a high-volume direct-to-consumer (DTC) Shopify store generating $20,000+ in monthly GMV. While customer acquisition through paid social ads was scaling predictably, the store suffered from high cart abandonment friction and customer churn due to slow manual response times on pre-purchase inquiries.

The Challenge & Failure Points

  • 30% Unrecovered Abandoned Carts: Standard generic reminder emails had an open rate below 14% and failed to address specific buyer objections (shipping times, sizing, return policies).
  • Support Bottleneck: The solo founder and a virtual assistant spent 15+ hours weekly answering repetitive FAQs ("Where is my order?", "Do you ship to X?"), with average reply times exceeding 2 hours.
  • Lost Sales Velocity: High-intent shoppers left the website without buying because questions went unanswered at the point of checkout.

The Engineering Solution

Gargi Kar designed and deployed a dual-pronged autonomous automation architecture using n8n, OpenAI GPT-4, and the Shopify Admin API:

// Autonomous Workflow Pipeline
1 Shopify Checkout Webhook fires on cart abandonment (>15 mins inactivity).
↳ n8n extracts customer browsing history, product SKUs, and checkout value.
2 GPT-4 synthesizes hyper-personalized recovery copy tailored to the exact products.
3 Automated multi-channel dispatch (dynamic email + optional SMS discount offer).
4 24/7 AI Chatbot handles instant pre-purchase queries, answering in 12 seconds.

The chatbot was grounded using few-shot prompt engineering with the store's full catalog knowledge, shipping timelines, and real-time inventory checks via API endpoints.
