Clinical Setting & Inbound Overload

A multi-location outpatient specialty medical clinic was overwhelmed by 50+ phone calls per day for routine appointment requests, insurance checks, and rescheduling. Front-desk receptionists were missing approximately 40% of peak call volume. Patients unable to get through during work hours simply booked appointments with competing clinics.

The 24/7 AI Scheduling Engine

Gargi Kar designed and deployed an automated patient interaction workflow using GPT-4, Twilio, Google Calendar API, and n8n:

// Autonomous Patient Scheduling Flow
1. Omni-Channel Inbound: Patient engages via website widget or SMS (day or night).
↳ GPT-4 performs friendly triage: symptom category, doctor availability, provider in-network check.
2. Real-Time Slot Locking: Direct API integration with Google Calendar finds matching openings.
3. Automated Verification: Requests insurance photo/ID and confirms copay parameters.
4. Smart Reminders: Automated 48h & 2h SMS reminders with one-click confirmation or reschedule.
