Firm Profile & Administrative Grind

A regional CPA firm managed tax filing and monthly accounting for over 120 small-to-medium enterprise clients. Certified accountants were spending 25 hours every week manually opening PDFs, re-typing receipt amounts into Excel spreadsheets, and reconciling bank statements line by line. During tax season, audit preparation took up to 10 working days per business, creating massive staff burnout.

The Autonomous Financial OCR & Reconciliation Engine

Gargi Kar designed and deployed an automated accounting pipeline utilizing Python, OCR APIs, n8n, and SQL databases:

// Document Intake & Ledger Matching Pipeline
1. Multi-Format Document Ingestion: Clients upload receipts/PDFs via email or portal.
↳ Python extracts structured data: vendor, tax ID, line items, sales tax, currency, and total.
2. AI Categorization & GL Tagging: Contextual LLM assigns general ledger account codes automatically.
3. Three-Way Reconciliation: Matches purchase order vs. vendor invoice vs. bank transaction record.
4. Exception Routing: Flags only anomalies (<3% discrepancy); 97% pass through untouched directly to QuickBooks/Xero.
