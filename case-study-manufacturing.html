Industrial Context & Critical Pain Point

A precision manufacturing plant with 50 employees produced engineered plastic and metallic assemblies. Inspectors relied on visual manual inspection on conveyor lines. Fatigue over 8-hour shifts resulted in an 8% quality failure rate. Defective batches frequently slipped past inspection into customer shipments, triggering severe penalties and risking full brand recalls.

Edge Vision & AI Detection Architecture

Gargi Kar designed and deployed an automated quality inspection pipeline utilizing Python, OpenCV, TensorFlow, and local Edge processing devices:

// Edge Vision Inspection Architecture
1. High-Speed Optical Capture: Industrial camera captures 30 frames/sec as parts traverse conveyor.
↳ OpenCV pre-processes contrast, lighting normalization, and bounding boxes in 14ms.
2. Convolutional Neural Net (TensorFlow): Analyzes surface micro-cracks, misalignment, and burrs.
3. Pneumatic Rejection Actuator: Instant hardware signal rejects defective part into quarantine bin.
4. Telemetry Dashboard: Real-time production analytics alert line supervisors to tool wear.
