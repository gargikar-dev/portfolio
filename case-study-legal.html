Client Context & Operational Inefficiency

A boutique corporate law firm with 5 active attorneys handled hundreds of routine NDAs, vendor master services agreements (MSAs), employment contracts, and court briefs each month. Highly paid attorneys were spending 12 hours per week copying, pasting, and manually cross-referencing boilerplate clauses from previous Word documents. This created burnout, high administrative overhead, and human error risks.

The Python & LangChain Engineering Pipeline

Gargi Kar designed and deployed an automated drafting system deployed within 10 days:

// Document Generation Architecture
1. Intake Form Integration: Client questionnaire captures deal points via secure Airtable form.
↳ Dynamic data validation checks jurisdictions, indemnity caps, and payment terms.
2. LangChain Clause Synthesis: Python engine matches terms against validated template clauses.
↳ Strict guardrails prevent LLM hallucination; terms are strictly bound to attorney-approved clauses.
3. Automated Formatting: Outputs pixel-perfect DOCX and PDF documents with legal numbering.
4. Attorney Audit Review: Dispatches directly into attorney's review queue ready for digital sign-off.
